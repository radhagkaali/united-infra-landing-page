SHEET 03 — SEQUENCE OF WORK

How a project moves

  1. 01

    Survey & plan

    Site survey, geotechnical review, and a constructability assessment before a single line is drawn.

  2. 02

    Design & permit

    Engineering, agency coordination, and permitting run in parallel to protect the schedule.

  3. 03

    Crews mobilize with a fixed sequence, daily safety checks, and weekly progress reporting to the owner.

    As-builts, warranty documentation, and an optional maintenance term once the asset is in service.
